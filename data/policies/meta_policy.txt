META PLATFORMS COMMUNITY STANDARDS - HATE SPEECH POLICY

We define hate speech as a direct attack against people on the basis of protected characteristics: race, ethnicity, national origin, disability, religious affiliation, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, and serious disease.

PROTECTED CHARACTERISTICS:
Meta protects individuals and groups based on the following characteristics:
- Race, ethnicity, national origin, religious affiliation
- Sexual orientation, gender identity, sex
- Disability, serious diseases, immigration status
- Age (when content is degrading or dismissive)

PROHIBITED CONTENT:
Tier 1 Violations (Most Severe):
- Dehumanizing speech comparing people to animals, insects, or objects
- Statements of inferiority claiming protected groups are physically, mentally, or morally deficient
- Expressions of contempt, disgust, or dismissal
- Cursing at a person based on protected characteristics

Tier 2 Violations:
- Generalizations stating that protected groups have negative characteristics
- Expressions of fearfulness about protected groups
- Statements supporting segregation or exclusion

ENFORCEMENT APPROACH:
Meta uses a combination of automated systems and human reviewers to detect and remove violating content. Our systems are trained on millions of examples and continuously updated.

Artificial Intelligence Detection:
- Proactive detection identifies 95% of hate speech before users report it
- Machine learning models analyze text, images, and video content
- Context-aware algorithms consider cultural and linguistic nuances

Human Review Process:
- Complex cases are escalated to specialized teams
- Cultural and regional expertise is applied to borderline content
- Appeals are reviewed by different teams to ensure consistency

CONSEQUENCES:
1. Content Removal: Violating posts are removed and users are notified
2. Account Restrictions: Temporary limitations on posting and sharing
3. Account Suspension: 30-day suspensions for severe or repeated violations
4. Permanent Removal: Accounts permanently disabled for extreme cases

EXCEPTIONS AND CONTEXT:
- Educational content about hate speech is allowed with proper context
- News reporting on hate incidents is permitted
- Artistic expression may be allowed if it doesn't promote harm
- Self-referential content (people referring to themselves) has different standards

APPEALS PROCESS:
Users can appeal enforcement decisions through the platform's oversight system. Independent review boards evaluate complex cases and policy interpretations.
